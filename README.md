# Business Lead Intelligence

A Python-based business lead intelligence platform designed to discover businesses, extract their contact information, validate data, and export qualified business leads.

## Project Goals

- Discover businesses using Serper Places Search.
- Collect structured business information.
- Visit official business websites.
- Extract email addresses, phone numbers, and addresses.
- Discover contact and about pages.
- Validate and normalize extracted information.
- Remove duplicate business records.
- Export results to Excel and CSV.

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Serper API
- Requests
- BeautifulSoup
- Phonenumbers
- Pandas
- OpenPyXL
- FastAPI (planned)
- React and TypeScript (planned)
